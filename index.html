Syllabus Mapping & Live Teacher Demo Guide

Smart Net-Zero Energy Green Building • Full Working IoT Demonstration

How to Present this Live to Your Teacher / Examiner:

Walk the examiner through the following 4 interactive tests in under 2 minutes. This proves it is a fully functioning smart engineering model, not just a static dollhouse!

TEST 1

1. Smart Daylight Harvesting (LDR Test)

Action: Click "Cover LDR Hand" (or drag Time of Day slider to Night 9 PM).

Daytime / Bright Sunlight → LDR Resistance Low → Controller detects > 300 Lux → Room LED stays OFF (0W Wastage)
Cover LDR with Hand → LDR Resistance Skyrockets → Controller detects Darkness → Room LED snaps ON Automatically!

What to say to the teacher: "Respected Teacher, here our LDR sensor continuously measures ambient lux. When natural sunlight enters the window, the microcontroller automatically keeps the interior LED off to conserve energy. When daylight drops or night falls, the lights activate instantly."

TEST 2

2. Occupancy Energy Preservation (PIR Sensor Test)

Action: Click "Toggle PIR Motion" to make a person leave or enter the room.

Room Empty (No Motion) → Even if it's pitch dark outside, LED turns OFF to save power!
Person enters room → Passive Infrared Sensor detects heat motion → LED turns ON.

What to say to the teacher: "This is our dual-condition energy-saving logic: Lights only consume power when it is DARK AND OCCUPIED. If nobody is inside, no power is ever wasted."

TEST 3

3. Smart Microclimate & Natural Cross-Ventilation (DHT11 + Fan + Servo Window)

Action: Click "Heat DHT11 (34°C)" button or drag Temperature slider above 28°C.

Temp < 28°C → Comfort Mode → Window CLOSED, Fan OFF
Temp ≥ 28°C → DHT11 signals Microcontroller → SG90 Servo opens Window 45° for natural breeze + DC Fan turns ON to extract hot air!

What to say to the teacher: "When the room overheats, the system first exploits passive cooling by motorized window louvers before running forced ventilation, drastically minimizing electricity consumption."

TEST 4

4. Green Roof Thermal Insulation & Rainwater Harvesting

Action: Toggle "Rainfall Weather" and click "Roof Strata" inspection.

Green Roof → Soil & vegetation absorb solar irradiance → Lowers roof surface temperature by 5°C-8°C via evapotranspiration.
Rainfall → Perimeter gutter collects runoff → Filter pipe channels water to ground tank → Reused for landscaping & flushing.

What to say to the teacher: "The green roof cuts HVAC cooling loads naturally, while the rainwater harvesting system turns stormwater runoff into a zero-cost water reservoir for our building."

UNIT 1

Energy-Efficient Building Architecture

Fundamentals of Net-Zero Energy Buildings (NZEB), orientation, energy benchmarking, building envelope insulation, and minimizing active loads.

  • Net-Zero Equation: Solar Energy Generated ≥ Total Energy Consumed
  • High thermal mass walls and dual-pane insulated glazing
UNIT 2

Solar Energy & Passive Cooling

Harnessing renewable solar photovoltaic (PV) generation and optimizing roof-tilt angles for maximum irradiance conversion.

  • Monocrystalline PV rooftop array with battery storage
  • Shading overhangs, thermal buffers, and passive stack ventilation
UNIT 3

Temperature, Indoor Air Quality & Ventilation

Closed-loop microclimate feedback systems using digital environmental sensors and hybrid passive-active cooling.

  • DHT11 digital temperature and humidity measurement
  • Servo-actuated motorized casement window for cross-breeze
  • Variable-speed low-power brushless DC ceiling fan
UNIT 4

Smart Lighting & Daylight Harvesting

Intelligent lighting control strategies integrating daylight availability and space occupancy sensing.

  • Light Dependent Resistor (LDR) analog voltage divider circuit
  • Pyroelectric Infrared (PIR) motion detection
  • Boolean logic gate automation: LIGHT = DARKNESS AND OCCUPIED
UNIT 5

Green Roofs, Sustainability & Rainwater Harvesting

Ecological site development, urban heat island mitigation, stormwater retention, and sustainable resource closed-loops.

  • Extensive vegetative green roof with drainage layer
  • Gravity-fed roof rainwater catchment, downspout & filtration tank
  • Non-potable water recycling for irrigation and sanitation

Component Type Microcontroller Pin Functional Logic Condition
LDR Sensor Analog Input Pin A0 Reads Lux. Triggers condition when Lux < 300 (Night / Darkness).
PIR Sensor Digital Input Pin D2 Detects human infrared heat movement. HIGH when occupied.
Room LED Lights Digital / PWM Output Pin D9 IF (LDR < 300 && PIR == HIGH) → TURN ON; ELSE OFF;
DHT11 Sensor Digital 1-Wire Pin D4 Samples temperature (°C) and humidity (%RH) every 1.5 seconds.
SG90 Servo Motor PWM Servo Pin D5 IF (Temp ≥ 28°C) write(90°) [OPEN]; ELSE write(0°) [CLOSED];
Cooling Fan Relay / MOSFET Pin D6 IF (Temp ≥ 28°C) digitalWrite(HIGH); ELSE LOW;
Arduino Uno R3 or ESP32 Wi-Fi Central microcontroller unit for sensor data processing and actuation.
6V 1W-2W Monocrystalline Mini Solar Panel Mounts on roof. Generates 5V-6V DC in bright sunlight for clean energy.
5mm LDR Light Dependent Resistor + 10kΩ Resistor Forms an analog voltage divider circuit to detect ambient daylight illumination.
HC-SR501 or AM312 PIR Infrared Motion Sensor Detects human presence within 3-7 meters via Fresnel lens.
DHT11 Digital Temperature & Humidity Sensor Measures room temperature from 0°C to 50°C (±2°C) and relative humidity.
TowerPro SG90 9g Micro Servo Motor Mechanically pivots the automatic cross-ventilation window sash open and closed.
5V DC Miniature Brushless Fan + 2N2222 Transistor / Relay Ventilates warm indoor air when ambient temperature exceeds comfortable setpoint.
